The book

Care is a commitment.
And a complication.

A family meal. A hunting tradition. A child’s trip to the zoo. Everyday choices can ask a great deal of our convictions.

Karen S. Emmerman explores the friction between commitment to animals and attachment to people. Through relational vegan ecofeminism, she asks how we can respond to ethical conflict with attention, empathy, and a willingness to repair.

For readers living these tensions—and those who want to understand them.

The author

Karen S.
Emmerman, PhD

Animal ethics. Ecofeminism.
Philosophy in everyday life.

Bringing philosophical questions into the world we live in.

Karen S. Emmerman is an affiliate instructor in philosophy at the University of Washington. Her work spans animal ethics, ecofeminism, and philosophy with children.

Based in Seattle, she also works with young people and educators, creating space for curiosity, critical thinking, and conversations about how we live together.
